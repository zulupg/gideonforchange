Policy Manifesto · Tewae-Siassi District

Our Pillars for New Hope & Vision

Grounded in transparent, action-led leadership — empowering families and communities while decentralizing power to the people of Tewae-Siassi.

Pillar 01 — Empower Families / Empower Communities

Strong Households. Strong Wards. Strong District.

Families are the foundation of Tewae-Siassi. This pillar focuses on practical support that lifts households and binds communities — primary healthcare, education pathways, women and youth opportunity, and village-level services that people can see and use.

  • Upgrade aid posts and sub-centers with medicines, solar power, and maternity support across wards.
  • Mobile clinics and referral support so remote families are not left behind.
  • Education and TVET pathways, youth skills, and family livelihood support.
  • Community water, sanitation, and safe gathering spaces that strengthen village life.
Pillar 02 — Decentralized Powers

Power Closer to the People of Tewae-Siassi

Decisions that affect villages should not sit only at the top. Decentralized powers means LLGs, ward committees, and local leaders share real authority and resources — so projects are planned with communities, delivered faster, and held accountable locally.

  • Stronger ward and LLG planning roles with clear funding channels for local priorities.
  • Transparent project lists so every community can see what is approved, funded, and delivered.
  • Community consultation before major works — roads, health posts, markets, and schools.
  • Local procurement where possible to keep jobs and cash circulating in Tewae-Siassi.
From Our New Vision — Resource Excellence

Maximise Our Resources — Responsibly

Work in excellence to maximise the value of our resources in an environmentally responsible way — so sustainable economic and social benefits flow to families and communities of Tewae-Siassi District, not only to outsiders.

  • Support cash-crop farmers, buying hubs, and fair market access for cocoa, coffee, and local produce.
  • SME and women’s micro-enterprise support so value stays in the district.
  • Environmentally responsible use of land and natural resources for long-term community benefit.
  • Feeder roads and basic infrastructure that link producers to markets without leaving villages behind.
Through Transparent & Action-Led Leadership

Promise Less. Deliver More. Report Openly.

New hope for Tewae-Siassi means leadership people can trust: clear priorities, open reporting, and action that matches the word. Transparent and action-led leadership is how we turn vision into results families can feel.

  • Public reporting on district projects, budgets, and timelines.
  • Regular community feedback sessions in wards — not only at election time.
  • Action plans with measurable targets for health, roads, youth, and livelihoods.
  • Zero tolerance for misuse of public resources — integrity first.
