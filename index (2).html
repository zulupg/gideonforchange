Policy Manifesto

4 Pillars of "Tauwei Siasi"

Pillar 01 — Primary Healthcare Upgrade

Full Medical Supply & Staffing in All Wards

Dr. Gideon S. Gelesi's top priority is equipping health aid posts and sub-centers in Tausia with essential medicine, solar power, and maternity care.

  • Mobile Medical Patrol Clinics for rural villages.
  • Emergency medical referral fund for patient transfers.
  • Clean water filtration systems installed at all health centers.
Pillar 02 — Essential Infrastructure

Connecting Remote Communities to Markets

Modernizing road networks to ensure cash crops reach urban centers efficiently and emergency vehicles can access all corners of the electorate.

  • All-weather graveling & drainage for key feeder roads.
  • Solar street lighting for main station centers.
Pillar 03 — Youth & Education

District Tuition Assistance & TVET

Investing directly in Tausia youth through tertiary fee assistance and practical trade skills training centers.

  • Annual Tertiary Education Subsidy Scheme for students.
  • Youth entrepreneurship innovation seed grants.
Pillar 04 — Micro-Economy & Agriculture

Empowering Local SMEs & Farmers

Creating direct buying points for local agricultural produce, supporting women's micro-finance groups, and enhancing local market infrastructure.

  • District SME incubation center for small business loans.
  • Subsidized farming tools & cocoa/coffee seedlings distribution.
